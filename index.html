Final Year Project · 2026

An inclusive recruitment platform for persons with disabilities

AbilityScan is a web-based recruitment platform designed to connect persons with disabilities with suitable job opportunities through a transparent matching process.

The platform uses a five-layer rule-based matching engine covering skills, physical, technology, work and sensory requirements.

AbilityScan also provides QR digital portfolios and explanations to help users understand their job-matching results.

  • Developed role-based features for persons with disabilities, employers and administrators.
  • Implemented the rule-based job-matching process.

IoT Group Project

Smart Garbage Collector

Group Project . Hardware Assembly

Robotics and Automation: Smart Garbage Collector

This IoT group project consists of a smart bin and a robotic garbage collector. The smart bin uses an ultrasonic sensor to detect movement nearby and automatically opens its lid using a motor.

When the bin reaches its maximum capacity, the system prevents the lid from opening and activates an indicator light to notify users that the bin is full.

The robotic garbage collector is then used to collect waste from the smart bin and transport it to designated waste collection centre.

My Role & Contribution

  • Assisted in assembling components for the smart bin and robotic garbage collector.
  • Supported hardware integration and prototype testing.
  • Collaborated with team members in preparing the project documentation.
